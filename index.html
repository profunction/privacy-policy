Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Stock Check mobile application (herein referred to as "Application") for mobile devices, developed by Bj Engineering and created as a Commercial service.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application collects and uses certain information automatically to provide, maintain, and improve our services. This includes infrastructure and operational support provided by third-party services.

The Application collects the following information:

  • Email address — used to sign in to your account.
  • User ID — a unique identifier generated by Firebase Authentication.
  • Stock-check and inventory records — the data you enter into the Application.

All of this data is stored in Google Firebase and is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to provide the Application's features and to manage your account. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services (Firebase)

The Application utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. Specifically, we use Google Firebase (including Firebase Authentication, Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics) to provide sign-in and data storage, to understand how the app is used, and to fix technical issues.

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics collect crash logs, device information and app usage data. Firebase may automatically collect data such as your device's Internet Protocol ("IP") address, device name, operating system version, the configuration of the app when utilizing the service, the time and date of your use of the service, and other statistics.

To learn more about how Google Firebase handles your data, please review their official privacy policy:

  • Google Privacy & Terms
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase

4. Cookies

Cookies are files with a small amount of data that are commonly used as anonymous unique identifiers. The Application does not use these "cookies" explicitly. However, the third-party code and libraries used by the Application (such as Firebase) may use "cookies" to collect information and improve their services.

5. Security

We value your trust in providing us your information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Account and Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. See how to delete your Stock Check account and data.

7. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
