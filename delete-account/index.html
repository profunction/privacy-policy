Delete Your Stock Check Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and data for the Stock Check app, developed by Bj Engineering.

How to Request Deletion

  1. From the email address you registered with, send an email to lawoozhe0101@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete my Stock Check account
  3. We will delete your account and data within 30 days and reply to confirm when it is done.

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your email address
  • Your user ID
  • All of your stock-check records stored in Firebase

Data That Will Be Retained

We do not retain any of your data after deletion, except in backups, which are purged within 30 days of deletion.

For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
